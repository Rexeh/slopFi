Renewals (3)

How it behaves

  • Looks ahead to the next close, not just today. A reminder could otherwise start between two closes and only show up with weeks lost.
  • Never blocks closing a month. "Look at it next close" ticks the step for this month only, like accepting a spending spike does.
  • The step is done once every renewal in the window is renewed or put off to next close.
  • The sidebar shows the same count as the step.

Why its own page

  • It's a to-do list, not an analysis, so it sits in the Review group next to Transactions.
  • The mortgage fix comes from Net worth. You edit it there, and nothing is entered twice.
  • "Paying now" uses the latest linked payment from your statements when there is one. Otherwise it's the price you recorded.
  • "Last time" shows the outcome of the last renewal: saved, or how much the price went up.
  • MOT and road tax use Done instead of Renew: they have a date and a cost, but no quotes.

How it behaves

  • Opens inside the row, not in a pop-up, so the dates and current price stay visible while you type.
  • "Saved" is the renewal quote minus what you chose, worked out as a yearly figure so monthly and yearly prices compare fairly.
  • The new end date defaults to a year later. Broadband and mobile might be 18 or 24 months, so it's editable.
  • A rise without switching shows as "up 12%" on the list.
  • Every past term is kept, so you can see the price over several years.

How it behaves

  • Suggestions fill in the form: name, price, category and the payee to match. You add the end date, because statements don't contain it.
  • The reminder time defaults by kind: 6 months for the mortgage fix, 3 months for everything else. Editable per renewal.
  • "Matches payments" links it to your statements, so "Paying now" stays up to date and a direct debit change shows up.
