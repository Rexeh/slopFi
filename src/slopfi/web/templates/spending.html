How to use Spending
  • Use averages over a period, not single months. Spending is lumpy; the 3-month average is the baseline, and 6 and 12 months show direction and seasonality.
  • Separate fixed from flexible. Fixed bills change by switching; flexible categories change by habit. Target them differently.
  • Mark genuine one-offs on Transactions so a holiday or a car repair does not look like a trend.
  • Set a target per category, then watch Over target.
  • The savings rate (net divided by income) is the single number that says whether the house-move fund is growing.
